Tooltip demo

One shared element for every anchor, its id is random per instance. Hover, focus with Tab, press Escape.

Placement

Markup only: data-tooltip and data-tooltip-placement, top by default. One listen() call serves every anchor, including ones added later.

Auto flip

The fixed buttons in the top and bottom right corners ask for the side that does not fit and get the opposite one.

Viewport clamp

Long text at the edges stays inside the viewport horizontally.

Multiline

Line breaks are kept (white-space: pre-line), long words wrap by theme.maxWidth.

Hides on scroll

Any scroll, including nested containers, hides the tooltip.

Manual control and theming

show(anchor, text) / hide(), configure({ offset }), destroy() and configure({ theme }), which regenerates the styles. Copy changes its data-tooltip on click - the open tooltip follows it.
